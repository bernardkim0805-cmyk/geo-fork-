# 1,000 Academic Questions in Ecology

A deliberately varied question bank for ecology coursework, seminars, literature reviews, research planning, and grammar-sensitive text processing. Questions span interrogative forms, clause structures, conditionals, ellipsis, quotation, punctuation, and register; adapt organisms, ecosystems, locations, and time scales to suit a project.
